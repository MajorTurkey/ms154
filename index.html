Download and install

Open this in Chrome. Tap the button, then open the downloaded file and tap Install. It updates the copy already on the tablet and keeps your movies.

YOLO detector. Boxes stay one quiet color. Same-direction traffic shows distance only. Oncoming speed uses the car-speed plug, and is held back when your speed or the pedal just changed. The hood is ignored. The left side is the oncoming lane.
