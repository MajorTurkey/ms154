What the camera does

  1. Hood. The still band at the bottom, up to where the road starts moving, is the hood. Boxes on it are ignored. A dashed line marks that line once it settles.
  2. Same direction. Cars going the same way are still recognized. Each one gets a box and a distance. They do not get a speed.
  3. Oncoming. The left side of a forward camera is checked more often. Only those cars get a speed: how fast the gap is shrinking, minus your speed.
  4. Close oncoming cars. Amber under about 28 meters, red under about 12 meters, and only while they are still closing. That is distance, not a countdown.

Your speed is the OBDLink CX

Distance works with no plug. An oncoming car's mph needs your speed from the OBDLink CX in the diagnostic port. GPS and lane dashes are only a fallback if the CX is not connected.

Plug it in, turn the car on, and pair within 5 minutes (PIN 123456 if the tablet asks). Close the OBDLink app and BimmerCode first. The CX talks to one app at a time.

This is source, not a new install file. The tablet keeps the old app until a new build is installed.

Why the old number was wrong

Seconds-to-the-screen treats the other car as if it is catching you from behind. Oncoming traffic is coming the other way, so both speeds add. Distance, plus your OBDLink CX speed, is the useful reading.
