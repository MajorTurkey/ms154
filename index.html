What the camera does

  1. Hood. The still band at the bottom is the hood. Boxes on it are ignored.
  2. Same direction. Still a box and a distance. No speed.
  3. Oncoming. Left side only gets a speed: closing rate minus your OBDLink CX speed.
  4. Close oncoming cars. Amber under about 28 meters, red under about 12 meters, only while still closing.
